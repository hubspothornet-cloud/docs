## Step 1: Match the cushion to the problem

Do not buy only by price. If slipping is the issue, start with an anti-slip product. If padding is the issue, start with a thicker cushion. If seat fit is the issue, compare product dimensions with your rowing machine seat.

## Step 2: Know that Hornet cushions are not only for Concept2

Hornet rowing machine cushions can fit Concept2 and many other common rowers, including Hydrow, Peloton, Merach, Sunny Health, and generic rowing machines. Seat shapes vary, so compare product dimensions and images with your machine before buying.

## Step 3: Consider thickness carefully

More padding can feel better for some rowers, but it can also change the feel and height of the seat. The Memory Foam Cushion is almost 2 inches thick and is the better starting point when maximum padding matters. The Anti Slip Cushion is the better starting point when grip and Regular or XL sizing matter. The Low-Profile Cushion is approximately 0.5 inch thick and is the better starting point when you want firm sports-grade PU foam padding with minimal change to seating height.

## Step 4: Keep technique and setup in the conversation

A cushion can add padding or grip, but seat pressure, sliding, or poor fit can also come from rowing volume, posture, seat position, clothing, or technique. A cushion is not a medical treatment and may not resolve pain, numbness, injury, or technique problems.

<Warning>
  If rowing causes persistent pain, numbness, or injury concerns, do not rely on a cushion alone. Review technique, machine setup, training volume, and seek qualified coaching or medical advice where appropriate.
</Warning>
