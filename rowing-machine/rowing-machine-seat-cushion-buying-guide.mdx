## How to choose

### 1. Decide how much added height you want

The Low-Profile and Regular Anti-Slip cushions are approximately 0.5 inch thick. The Anti-Slip XL is 0.63 inch thick. The Thick Memory Foam Cushion is 1.88 inches thick.

Any cushion raises the rower slightly. Choose a lower-profile option when maintaining closer contact with the original seat height matters. Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion when maximum padding matters more than minimizing added height.

### 2. Decide which grip design you prefer

The Low-Profile Cushion uses extra-strong anti-slip fabric on the bottom. The Anti-Slip Cushion uses silicone printing on both the top and bottom. The Thick Memory Foam Cushion uses silicone printing on the bottom.

All three are strap-free and rest directly on the rowing-machine seat.

### 3. Choose seat coverage

Choose Anti-Slip Regular for standard coverage. Choose Anti-Slip XL for a larger 12.5 by 9.75-inch surface and slightly more padding. Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion for the widest surface in this comparison.

### 4. Compare your rowing-machine seat dimensions

Hornet cushions are used with many popular rowing machines, including Concept2, Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, Sunny Health, Merach, and other machines with compatible seat dimensions.

The Low-Profile Cushion was designed around the Concept2 seat shape. Customers have also reported using it on Hydrow, Hydrow Wave, WaterRower, Aviron, Sunny Health, NordicTrack, ProForm, Rogue Echo Rower, Procor, and Fitness Reality machines.

The Thick Memory Foam Cushion is designed for many popular rowers, including Concept2, Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, and Sunny Health. Customers have also reported use on Aviron, NordicTrack, ProForm, Pooboo, and other rowers.

These compatibility examples are not a universal fit guarantee. Compare the selected cushion dimensions and shape with your exact seat before ordering.

## FAQs

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has the most padding?

The Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's thickest option at 1.88 inches.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion changes seating height the least?

The Low-Profile Cushion and Regular Anti-Slip Cushion are both approximately 0.5 inch thick. Choose the Low-Profile Cushion for firm PU foam and an anti-slip fabric bottom. Choose Anti-Slip Regular for silicone grip on both sides.

### What is the difference between Anti-Slip Regular and XL?

Regular measures 11.5 by 9 by 0.5 inches. XL measures 12.5 by 9.75 by 0.63 inches, giving you a larger seating surface and slightly more padding.

### Which cushion has grip on both sides?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has silicone printing on both the top and bottom.

### Do the cushions attach with straps or clips?

No. The Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam cushions are strap-free and rest directly on the rowing-machine seat.

### Is the Thick Memory Foam Cushion soft or firm?

It has a supportive, firmer memory-foam feel. The foam gradually conforms during use and is designed to provide substantial padding without immediately bottoming out.

### Why is the Thick Memory Foam Cushion wider?

Its 13.25 by 10.25-inch shape provides a broader seating surface to support the added 1.88-inch thickness.

### Do Hornet cushions fit rowing machines other than Concept2?

Yes. Hornet cushions are designed for use with many popular and generic rowers. Compare the selected cushion dimensions with your specific machine seat before ordering.

### Can the Thick Memory Foam Cushion be used on a recumbent exercise bike?

Some customers have reported using it on recumbent exercise bikes. Bike-seat shapes vary, so compare the 13.25 by 10.25-inch cushion shape with your seat before ordering.

### Has Dark Horse Rowing featured a Hornet seat pad?

Yes. Dark Horse Rowing included a Hornet Water Sports Seat Pad in its 2020 “Top 10 Must-Have Accessories for the Rower” video.

### Can a rowing cushion correct technique or treat an injury?

No. Treat a cushion as a seat and training accessory. Technique, machine setup, training load, and persistent physical concerns should be evaluated separately.
