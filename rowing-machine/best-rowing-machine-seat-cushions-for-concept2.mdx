## How to choose

### Choose anti-slip if sliding is the problem

The Anti Slip Rowing Machine Cushion is the best starting point when you care about grip, staying in place, and having Regular or XL size options.

### Choose Memory Foam When Maximum Padding Is the Priority

The Memory Foam Cushion is the best starting point when you want the thickest Hornet cushion option. It is almost 2 inches thick.

That added thickness raises your seating position more than a lower-profile cushion. Choose it when maximum padding matters more than minimizing the change in seat height.

### Choose the Low-Profile Cushion When Seat Height Matters

The Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is approximately 11.5 by 9 by 0.5 inches and uses a sports-grade PU foam core with an anti-slip bottom. Choose it when you want firm padding, a strap-free design, and less change to seating height than the nearly 2-inch Memory Foam Cushion.

It was designed around the shape of a Concept2 seat. Customers have also reported using it on Hydrow, Hydrow Wave, WaterRower, Aviron, Sunny Health, NordicTrack, ProForm, Rogue Echo, Procor, and Fitness Reality rowing machines. Compare dimensions before ordering.

## FAQs

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has the most padding?

The Memory Foam Cushion is the best starting point for added padding because it is Hornet's thickest version and almost 2 inches thick.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has XL sizing?

The Anti Slip Rowing Machine Cushion has Regular and XL variants.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion is the lowest-profile option?

The Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is approximately 0.5 inch thick. It is designed for firm padding and anti-slip placement with less change to seating height than the nearly 2-inch Memory Foam Cushion.

### Does the Low-Profile Cushion attach with straps?

No. It rests directly on the seat and uses an anti-slip bottom, making it easy to remove or move between rowing machines.

### How do I clean the Low-Profile Cushion?

Wipe it with a damp cloth and allow it to air dry.

### Do Hornet rowing cushions fit Hydrow, Peloton, Merach, or Sunny Health rowers?

Hornet rowing machine cushions can fit many common branded and generic rowers, including Hydrow, Peloton, Merach, and Sunny Health. Compare product dimensions with your machine seat before buying.

### Can a cushion fix rowing pain?
