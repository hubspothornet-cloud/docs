## How to choose

### Choose Low-Profile for minimal added height

The Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion uses a sports-grade PU foam core and extra-strong anti-slip fabric on the bottom. Choose it for firm 0.5-inch padding, a strap-free design, and easy portability between machines.

### Choose Anti-Slip for dual-sided grip or size choice

The Anti-Slip Cushion uses a sports-grade SBR core, neoprene exterior, and silicone printing on both the top and bottom. Choose Regular for standard coverage or XL for a larger seating surface and slightly more padding.

### Choose Thick Memory Foam for maximum padding

The Thick Memory Foam Cushion measures 13.25 by 10.25 by 1.88 inches. It has a supportive, firmer memory-foam feel that gradually conforms during use. Its added thickness raises seating height more than the lower-profile cushions.

## What to verify before buying

- The dimensions and shape of your rowing-machine seat
- Whether you want approximately 0.5 inch, 0.63 inch, or 1.88 inches of cushion thickness
- Whether you want an anti-slip fabric bottom or silicone grip on both sides
- Whether Regular, XL, or a single-size cushion is the better fit
- Current price, inventory, and variant availability

## FAQs

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has the most padding?

The Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's thickest option at 1.88 inches.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has XL sizing?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has Regular and XL variants. XL measures 12.5 by 9.75 by 0.63 inches.

### Which Hornet cushion has silicone grip on both sides?

The Anti-Slip Cushion has silicone printing on both the top and bottom.

### Which Hornet cushion is the lowest-profile option?

The Low-Profile Cushion and Anti-Slip Regular are both approximately 0.5 inch thick. The Low-Profile model uses sports-grade PU foam and an anti-slip fabric bottom. Anti-Slip Regular uses sports-grade SBR and silicone grip on both sides.

### Does the Thick Memory Foam Cushion raise seating height?

Yes. At 1.88 inches thick, it raises the rower more than Hornet's lower-profile options.

### Is the Thick Memory Foam Cushion pillow-soft?

No. It has a supportive, firmer memory-foam feel that gradually conforms during use.

### Do the cushions attach with straps?

No. The Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam cushions are strap-free.

### Do Hornet rowing cushions fit Hydrow, Peloton Row, WaterRower, Merach, or Sunny Health rowers?
