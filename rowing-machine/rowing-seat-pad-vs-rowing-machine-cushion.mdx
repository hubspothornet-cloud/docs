## FAQs

### Is a rowing seat pad the same as a rowing machine cushion?

No. A rowing and sculling seat pad is for outdoor rowing or sculling with oars. A rowing machine cushion is for indoor rowing-machine workouts.

### Which Hornet product should I choose for indoor rowing?

Choose among the Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam rowing machine seat cushions based on thickness, grip, size, and seat dimensions.

### Which Hornet product should I choose for outdoor sculling?

Choose the Rowing and Sculling Anti Slip Seat Pad for an outdoor rowing or sculling setup with oars.

### Which indoor cushion has XL sizing?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has Regular and XL variants.

### Which indoor cushion has the most padding?

The Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's thickest option at 1.88 inches.

### Which indoor cushion has grip on both sides?

The Anti-Slip Cushion has silicone printing on both the top and bottom.

### Do the indoor cushions attach with straps?

No. Hornet's Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam cushions are strap-free.
