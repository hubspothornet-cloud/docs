## Best gift by cushion preference

Choose the Low-Profile Cushion for a rower who wants firm padding, portability, and minimal change to seating height.

Choose the Anti-Slip Cushion when the rower wants silicone grip on both sides or a choice between Regular and XL seat coverage.

Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion when maximum padding and a wider seating surface are the priorities. It is 1.88 inches thick, so it raises seating height more than the lower-profile options.

## Best gift for non-Concept2 rowers

Hornet rowing machine cushions are designed for use with many popular rowing machines, including Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, Sunny Health, Merach, and other machines with compatible seat dimensions. Compare the product dimensions with the recipient's rowing-machine seat before buying.

## Best gift when size is uncertain

The Anti-Slip Cushion is the best starting point when size choice matters because it comes in Regular and XL. When you do not know the rower's machine, seat dimensions, or padding preference, a Hornet Gift Card is the safest option.

## FAQs

### What is the best rowing machine gift?

A Hornet rowing machine seat cushion can be a practical gift when the rower model, seat dimensions, and padding preference are known.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has XL sizing?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has Regular and XL variants. XL measures 12.5 by 9.75 by 0.63 inches.

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has the most padding?

The Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's thickest option at 1.88 inches.

### Which cushion is best when the recipient wants minimal added seat height?

The Low-Profile Cushion and Anti-Slip Regular are both approximately 0.5 inch thick. Choose Low-Profile for firm PU foam and an anti-slip fabric bottom. Choose Anti-Slip Regular for silicone grip on both sides.

### What should I buy when I do not know the rower's seat dimensions?

Choose a Hornet Gift Card or confirm the machine model and seat dimensions before selecting a cushion.

### Do Hornet rowing cushions fit machines other than Concept2?

Yes. They are designed for use with many popular rowers when the seat dimensions and shape are compatible. Compare measurements before ordering.
