## Product positioning

Choose this cushion when dual-sided grip, lower-profile support, or a choice of seat coverage matters most.

- Choose Regular for standard coverage.
- Choose XL for a larger seating surface and slightly more padding.
- Choose the Low-Profile Cushion for a single-size sports-grade PU foam design with an anti-slip fabric bottom.
- Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion when maximum padding and a wider seating surface are the priorities.

## Fit and compatibility

The product is designed for use with many popular rowing machines, including Concept2, Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, Sunny Health, Merach, and other machines with compatible seat dimensions.

Rowing-machine seat shapes vary by brand and model. Compare the selected Regular or XL dimensions with the exact seat before ordering.

## Seating-height tradeoff

Regular is 0.5 inch thick and XL is 0.63 inch thick. Both are lower-profile options and raise seating height less than Hornet's 1.88-inch Thick Memory Foam Cushion.
