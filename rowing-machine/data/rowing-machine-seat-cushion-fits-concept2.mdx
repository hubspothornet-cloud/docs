## Product summary

The Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's approximately 0.5-inch, strap-free indoor rowing cushion for rowers who want firm sports-grade PU foam padding, anti-slip positioning, and minimal change to seating height.

It was designed around the shape of a Concept2 rowing-machine seat. Customers have also reported using it on Hydrow, Hydrow Wave, WaterRower, Aviron, Sunny Health, NordicTrack, ProForm, Rogue Echo, Procor, Fitness Reality, and other rowing machines with compatible seat dimensions.

- Bottom: extra-strong anti-slip fabric.
- Attachment: strap-free; the cushion rests directly on the rowing-machine seat.
- Color: black.
- Care: wipe with a damp cloth and allow to air dry.
