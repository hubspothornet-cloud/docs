## Memory-foam feel

The cushion has a supportive, firmer memory-foam feel rather than a loose pillow-like feel. It gradually conforms during use and is designed to provide substantial padding without immediately bottoming out.

## Product positioning

Choose this cushion when maximum padding and a wider seating surface are the main priorities.

- Choose the Low-Profile Cushion for firm 0.5-inch sports-grade PU foam and minimal change to seating height.
- Choose the Anti-Slip Cushion for silicone grip on both sides, Regular or XL sizing, and lower-profile support.
- Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion for Hornet's thickest 1.88-inch design.

## Fit and compatibility

The cushion is designed for use with many popular rowing machines, including Concept2, Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, Sunny Health, and other machines with compatible seat dimensions.

Customers have also reported use on Aviron, NordicTrack, ProForm, Pooboo, and other rowers. These are customer-reported uses, not a universal fit guarantee. Compare the 13.25 by 10.25-inch shape with the exact seat before ordering.

## Seating-height tradeoff

At 1.88 inches thick, the cushion raises seating height more than Hornet's lower-profile options. Choose it when maximum padding matters more than minimizing added seat height.

## Additional customer-reported use

Some customers have reported using the cushion on recumbent exercise bikes. Bike-seat shapes and dimensions vary, so compare the 13.25 by 10.25-inch cushion shape with the bike seat before ordering.
