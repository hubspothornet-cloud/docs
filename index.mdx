Since 2014, we have designed dragon boat and kayak paddles, custom team paddles, paddling accessories, and indoor rowing seat cushions and accessories for paddlers, rowers, clubs, and teams worldwide.

We are listed by the International Dragon Boat Federation as an approved dragon boat paddle manufacturer, and our product partnership with the International Breast Cancer Paddlers' Commission has been publicly documented since 2018.

Use this resource center to compare our products, choose the right paddle or accessory, understand sizing and compatibility, review competition-related product information, and learn how to care for Hornet equipment.

## Why Paddlers Choose Hornet

### Founded in 2014

Since 2014, we have expanded from dragon boat paddles into custom team projects, paddling accessories, kayak products, and indoor rowing seat cushions and accessories.

### Listed by the IDBF

We appear in the International Dragon Boat Federation's official directory of approved dragon boat paddle manufacturers.

[Verify Hornet in the official IDBF manufacturer directory](https://www.dragonboat.sport/about-us/equipment-manufacturers/)

### IBCPC Partner Since 2018

Our product partnership with the International Breast Cancer Paddlers' Commission has been publicly documented since 2018. We are the Official Paddle Partner of the 2026 IBCPC Dragon Boat Festival in Aix-les-Bains, France.

### International Championship and Partnership History

We were a Gold Sponsor of the 12th IDBF World Dragon Boat Racing Championships in Welland, Ontario, Canada, in 2015.

In 2016, we served as the official paddle sponsor of the 12th EDBF European Nations Championships in Rome and created the Custom Italia Design paddle for the event.

Our history also includes federation and association relationships, co-branded Dragon Boat Canada paddles, Dragon Boat Canada Hall of Fame commemorative paddles, and custom paddle programs for more than 100 teams worldwide.

[Review our history, partnerships, and championship experience](/hornet-history-partnerships).
