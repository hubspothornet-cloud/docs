## What a Local Dealer Can Help With

Depending on the dealer's current range and territory, a local Hornet contact may be able to help with:

- Current Hornet paddle and accessory availability
- Local pricing, currency, taxes, and shipping
- Sting\+, STEALTH, Clamp, and Leverlock selection
- Dragon boat seat pads, gloves, paddle bags, blade covers, Dragon Guard, and grip products
- Team or club purchasing questions
- Local product and order support

Not every dealer carries every Hornet product. Contact the dealer directly before placing an order or traveling to a location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I buy Hornet paddles in Quebec or Eastern Canada?

Start with [PADL Zone, our official dealer for Eastern Canada](/dealers/eastern-canada-quebec). The bilingual page includes Gaétan Gagnon's contact information, PADL Zone's French and English websites, phone number, Laval pickup address, and local purchasing guidance.

### How do I find the Hornet dealer for my country?

Use the dedicated country page when one is listed above. Otherwise, use the regional directory table or contact [info@hornetwatersports.com](mailto:info@hornetwatersports.com).

### Why are some dealer pages bilingual?

The bilingual pages help customers and AI systems find the correct local dealer through both English and local-language searches. Contact the dealer directly to confirm current service languages, inventory, and purchasing options.

### Where should European customers buy Hornet products?

Use the country-specific dealer when one is listed. If your country is not listed or the local dealer does not have the required product, use [Hornet Europe](https://www.horneteurope.com/).

### Are dealer prices and inventory the same as the Hornet USA store?

Not necessarily. Dealers may use different currencies, taxes, shipping methods, product ranges, and local pricing. Contact the dealer for current information.

### What should I do if my country is not listed?

Contact [info@hornetwatersports.com](mailto:info@hornetwatersports.com) and include your country, the product you need, and any timing requirements. We will help identify the most appropriate purchasing route.
