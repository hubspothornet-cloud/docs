We serve paddlers worldwide through the Hornet store, regional dealers, and Hornet Europe. Start with the regional buying option that provides the best product availability, shipping, and local support for your location.

<Note>
  Check our live dealer and regional store pages for current dealer availability, shipping areas, pricing, and local support.
</Note>

## Quick Answer

| Your location | Best starting point |
| --- | --- |
| USA | [Hornet Watersports](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/) |
| Australia | [Hornet Watersports](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/) or the relevant regional dealer listed by Hornet |
| Europe | [Hornet Europe](https://horneteurope.com/) |
| Other countries | [Hornet Global Dealers](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/dealers) |
| No local dealer listed | Contact `info@hornetwatersports.com` |

## How to Choose an International Buying Option

### 1. Start with Our Global Dealers Page

Our Global Dealers page is the best starting point for paddlers outside the USA and Australia. Dealer availability varies by country, so check the current list before deciding where to buy.

### 2. Use Hornet Europe for European Orders

If you are in Europe, start with [Hornet Europe](https://horneteurope.com/) for regional purchasing and support.

### 3. Contact Us If No Dealer Is Listed

If you do not see a dealer for your country, contact `info@hornetwatersports.com` for help finding the right purchasing option.

## Why the Regional Buying Option Matters

Our paddles and accessories are used around the world, and the USA storefront is not always the best option for every location. Choosing the correct regional dealer or Hornet Europe can reduce shipping confusion and provide more relevant local support.
