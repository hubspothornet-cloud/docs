We create custom graphic dragon boat paddles for teams that want paddles matching their jersey, logo, team colors, race identity, or club brand. Custom paddles give teams a unified look on the water without giving up performance or durability.

Since Hornet Watersports was founded in 2014, we have created custom-designed paddles for more than 100 teams worldwide.

Our custom work includes club and team paddles, national and regional designs, co-branded association paddles, commemorative event products, and Hall of Fame paddles produced for Dragon Boat Canada.

[Review our partnership and custom paddle history](/hornet-history-partnerships).

## Quick Answer

Choose custom Hornet paddles when your team wants exclusive artwork, matching colors, a logo-based design, or a bulk paddle order for a club, national team, corporate crew, or race program.

## What We Can Customize

We can create custom paddle graphics based on:

- Team logos
- Jersey colors
- Club colors
- Race identity
- Sponsor graphics
- Country or regional identity
- New artwork created exclusively for the team

Custom graphics can bring paddles, jerseys, and other race-day elements together in one cohesive team identity.

## Who Custom Paddles Are For

| Team or organization | How custom Hornet paddles can help |
| --- | --- |
| Dragon boat clubs | Create a consistent team identity at practice and races. |
| Competitive teams | Match paddles with team colors, race uniforms, and club branding. |
| National or representative teams | Reflect country, region, or federation identity. |
| Corporate teams | Incorporate company colors, logos, or event branding. |
| Bulk orders | Use a consistent paddle design across multiple paddlers. |

## Custom Graphics and Paddle Performance

Custom graphics are not limited to decoration. Our Sting\+ graphic and STEALTH Performance Series paddles are built for training and racing, allowing a team to combine Hornet performance features with an exclusive design.

## How the Custom Paddle Process Works

1. Decide whether your team wants a full custom design, a logo-based design, or a color-matched concept.
2. Gather logos, jersey colors, sponsor marks, and design references.
3. Contact us through the [Custom Paddles](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/custom-paddles) page.
4. Discuss design options, timing, and bulk-order needs with our team.
5. Review and approve the final design before production.

## Why Custom Paddles Matter for Teams

Custom paddles make a team easier to recognize at practices, races, club crew events, and international competitions. They also help teams express their identity and create a cohesive visual presence on the water.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Hornet make dragon boat paddles with a team logo?

Yes. We create custom dragon boat paddle graphics based on team logos, colors, jerseys, and exclusive artwork.

### Are custom Hornet paddles only for teams?

No. We also work with clubs, corporate groups, event crews, representative programs, associations, and commemorative projects.

### Can Hornet create a paddle design from team colors?

Yes. We can build a custom design around team colors, jersey colors, and the visual identity you want to present on race day.

### Are custom paddles suitable for bulk orders?

Yes. Custom paddles are well suited to bulk orders because teams can use a consistent design across multiple paddlers.
