## Performance Beyond the Graphics

Our paddles are visually distinctive, but the design is only part of the product. Sting\+ graphic and STEALTH Performance Series paddles combine carbon construction, PMI foam cores, Diamond Edge reinforcement, removable non-slip grip covers, defined adjustable ranges, and IDBF approval under license 3525.

We are also listed in the International Dragon Boat Federation's official directory of approved dragon boat paddle manufacturers. [Review our IDBF approval and license information](/dragon-boat/idbf-approved-dragon-boat-paddles).

Founded in 2014, we have built a documented record through IDBF-approved paddle manufacturing, international championship sponsorships, federation and association relationships, and custom paddle programs for more than 100 teams worldwide.

## Sting\+ Graphic Paddles

Choose Sting\+ graphic paddles when you want performance construction with full-color paddle artwork. They use a PMI foam inner core, 3K carbon fiber exterior, 3K twill carbon fiber weave, matte finish, full-color graphics on both sides, a reinforced Diamond Edge blade, a 2-piece shaft, and a removable non-slip grip cover.

## STEALTH Performance Series

Choose STEALTH when you want stiffness, responsiveness, durability, and a modern carbon look for high-level training and racing. STEALTH paddles use marbleized carbon construction, a high-performance PMI foam core, a reinforced Diamond Edge blade, a glossy finish with metallic accents, a 2-piece adjustable shaft, and a removable non-slip grip cover.

## Custom Paddles for Teams

We create custom graphic paddles for teams that want paddles matching jerseys, team colors, logos, sponsors, or club identity. Custom paddles work well for bulk orders and teams that want a cohesive race-day look.

## Why Beginners Can Choose Hornet

Our adjustable paddles give newer paddlers room to refine their fit over time. Clamp covers 45 in to 51 in, while Leverlock covers 43 in to 51 in, providing flexibility for learning, training, or shared team equipment.

## Why Competitive Paddlers Can Choose Hornet

Competitive paddlers can compare IDBF approval, carbon construction, stiffness, responsiveness, handle weight, and adjustable range. Choose STEALTH for marbleized carbon construction and a racing-focused finish. Choose Sting\+ graphic paddles when you want comparable performance features with full-color artwork.

A Hornet adjustable paddle can be a premium gift when you know the recipient's handle and fit preferences. When those details are unknown, accessories such as Dragon Guard, paddle cleaner, grip tape, paddling gloves, seat pads, blade covers, and paddle bags are easier choices.
