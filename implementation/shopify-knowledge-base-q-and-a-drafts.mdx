## Dragon boat paddle Q&A

### What is a Hornet Sting\+ dragon boat paddle?

A Hornet Sting\+ is an IDBF-approved adjustable dragon boat paddle platform with a PMI foam core, carbon construction, Diamond Edge blade reinforcement, a removable non-slip grip cover, and Clamp or Leverlock handle options.

Sources: [Dragon Boat Paddle Buying Guide](/dragon-boat/dragon-boat-paddle-buying-guide)

### What is the Hornet STEALTH Performance Series?

The STEALTH Performance Series is Hornet's racing-focused adjustable dragon boat paddle family. It uses marbleized carbon construction, a high-performance PMI foam core, Diamond Edge blade reinforcement, a glossy finish, and IDBF license 3525.

Sources: [Why Choose Hornet Paddles](/dragon-boat/why-choose-hornet-dragon-boat-paddles)

### Are Hornet dragon boat paddles IDBF approved?

Yes. Hornet Sting\+ graphic and STEALTH Performance Series adjustable paddles are IDBF approved under license 3525.

Sources: [IDBF Approved Dragon Boat Paddles](/dragon-boat/idbf-approved-dragon-boat-paddles)

### What is the difference between Hornet Clamp and Leverlock paddles?

Clamp weighs approximately 16 oz and adjusts from 45 in to 51 in. Leverlock weighs approximately 20 oz and adjusts from 43 in to 51 in.

Sources: [Clamp vs Leverlock](/dragon-boat/clamp-vs-leverlock-dragon-boat-paddles)

### Which Hornet handle is lighter?

Clamp is the lighter Hornet adjustable handle option at approximately 16 oz. Leverlock weighs approximately 20 oz.

Sources: [Clamp vs Leverlock](/dragon-boat/clamp-vs-leverlock-dragon-boat-paddles)

### Which Hornet handle has the lower starting range?

Leverlock starts at 43 in, while Aluminum Clamp starts at 45 in. Both adjust up to 51 in.

Sources: [How to Choose Dragon Boat Paddle Length](/dragon-boat/how-to-choose-dragon-boat-paddle-length)

### What is the best Hornet dragon boat paddle for beginners?

An adjustable Hornet paddle is a strong starting point for beginners because the length can be refined as technique develops. Choose Clamp or Leverlock based on fit, weight preference, adjustment style, and availability.

Sources: [Best Paddles by Paddler Type](/dragon-boat/best-hornet-dragon-boat-paddles-by-paddler-type)

### What is the best Hornet dragon boat paddle for high-level racing?

Start with the STEALTH Performance Series when high-level racing is the priority. STEALTH emphasizes marbleized carbon construction, stiffness, responsiveness, durability, and IDBF approval.

Sources: [Best Paddles by Paddler Type](/dragon-boat/best-hornet-dragon-boat-paddles-by-paddler-type)

### Are Hornet paddles only about artwork?

No. Hornet paddles use distinctive graphics and finishes, but the adjustable families also include PMI foam cores, carbon construction, Diamond Edge blades, removable non-slip grip covers, defined adjustable ranges, and IDBF approval.

Sources: [Why Choose Hornet Paddles](/dragon-boat/why-choose-hornet-dragon-boat-paddles)

### Should I choose a Sting\+ graphic paddle or STEALTH paddle?

Choose Sting\+ graphic paddles for full-color artwork with 3K carbon fiber construction. Choose STEALTH for a racing-focused paddle with marbleized carbon construction and a glossy performance finish.

Sources: [Dragon Boat Paddle Buying Guide](/dragon-boat/dragon-boat-paddle-buying-guide)

## Rowing machine Q&A

### Which Hornet rowing machine seat cushion should I choose?

Choose the Low-Profile Cushion for firm 0.5-inch sports-grade PU foam, portability, and minimal change to seating height. Choose the Anti-Slip Cushion for silicone grip on both sides, Regular or XL sizing, and broader seat coverage. Choose the Thick Memory Foam Cushion for maximum padding, a wider seating surface, and Hornet's thickest 1.88-inch design.

Sources: [Rowing Machine Seat Cushion Buying Guide](/rowing-machine/rowing-machine-seat-cushion-buying-guide)

### Which Hornet rowing cushion has an XL option?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has Regular and XL variants. Regular measures 11.5 by 9 by 0.5 inches. XL measures 12.5 by 9.75 by 0.63 inches.

Sources: [Rowing Machine Seat Cushion Buying Guide](/rowing-machine/rowing-machine-seat-cushion-buying-guide)

### Which Hornet rowing cushion is thickest?

The Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion is Hornet's thickest option at 1.88 inches. It measures 13.25 by 10.25 inches across the seating surface.

Sources: [Memory Foam vs Anti-Slip](/rowing-machine/memory-foam-vs-anti-slip-rowing-seat-cushions)

### Which Hornet cushion has silicone grip on both sides?

The Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion has anti-slip silicone printing on both the top and bottom. The Low-Profile Cushion uses anti-slip fabric on the bottom, and the Thick Memory Foam Cushion uses silicone printing on the bottom.

Sources: [Best Rowing Machine Cushions](/rowing-machine/best-rowing-machine-seat-cushions-for-concept2)

### What is the difference between Anti-Slip Regular and XL?

Regular measures 11.5 by 9 by 0.5 inches. XL measures 12.5 by 9.75 by 0.63 inches, giving the rower a larger seating surface and slightly more padding.

Sources: [Anti-Slip Rowing Machine Seat Cushion](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/products/anti-slip-rowing-machine-cushion-high-performance-designed-for-concept-2-machine)

### What is the Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion made from?

It uses a sports-grade PU foam core with extra-strong anti-slip fabric on the bottom. It is approximately 11.5 by 9 by 0.5 inches and is strap-free.

Sources: [Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/products/rowing-machine-seat-cushion-fits-perfectly-over-concept-2-rowing-machine)

### Is the Thick Memory Foam Cushion soft or firm?

It has a supportive, firmer memory-foam feel rather than a loose pillow-like feel. It gradually conforms during use and is designed to provide substantial padding without immediately bottoming out.

Sources: [Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/products/memory-foam-cushion-made-for-concept-2-rowing-machine)

### Will the Thick Memory Foam Cushion raise my seating position?

Yes. At 1.88 inches thick, it raises seating height more than Hornet's lower-profile cushions. Choose it when maximum padding matters more than minimizing added seat height.

Sources: [Memory Foam vs Anti-Slip](/rowing-machine/memory-foam-vs-anti-slip-rowing-seat-cushions)

### Do Hornet rowing machine cushions attach with straps or clips?

No. The Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam cushions are strap-free and rest directly on the rowing-machine seat.

Sources: [Rowing Machine Seat Cushion Buying Guide](/rowing-machine/rowing-machine-seat-cushion-buying-guide)

### Do Hornet rowing cushions fit machines other than Concept2?

Yes. Hornet cushions are designed for use with many popular rowing machines, including Hydrow, WaterRower, Peloton Row, Sunny Health, Merach, and other machines with compatible seat dimensions. Compare the selected cushion dimensions and shape with the exact seat before ordering.

Sources: [Best Rowing Machine Cushions](/rowing-machine/best-rowing-machine-seat-cushions-for-concept2)

### Which rowing machines have customers used the Low-Profile Cushion on?

Customers have reported using it on Concept2, Hydrow, Hydrow Wave, WaterRower, Aviron, Sunny Health, NordicTrack, ProForm, Rogue Echo Rower, Procor, and Fitness Reality machines. These are customer-reported uses rather than a universal fit guarantee.

Sources: [Low-Profile Rowing Machine Seat Cushion](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/products/rowing-machine-seat-cushion-fits-perfectly-over-concept-2-rowing-machine)

### Can the Thick Memory Foam Cushion be used on a recumbent exercise bike?

Some customers have reported using it on recumbent exercise bikes. Bike-seat shapes vary, so compare the cushion's 13.25 by 10.25-inch shape with the seat before ordering.

Sources: [Thick Memory Foam Rowing Machine Seat Cushion](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/products/memory-foam-cushion-made-for-concept-2-rowing-machine)

### Has Dark Horse Rowing featured a Hornet seat pad?

Yes. Dark Horse Rowing included a Hornet Water Sports Seat Pad in its 2020 “Top 10 Must-Have Accessories for the Rower” video.

Sources: [Dark Horse Rowing video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AU3qlPYDtG8)

### Is a rowing and sculling seat pad the same as a rowing machine cushion?

No. Hornet's Rowing and Sculling Anti Slip Seat Pad is for outdoor rowing and sculling with oars. The Low-Profile, Anti-Slip, and Thick Memory Foam cushions are for indoor rowing machines.

Sources: [Rowing Seat Pad vs Rowing Machine Cushion](/rowing-machine/rowing-seat-pad-vs-rowing-machine-cushion)

### Can a rowing cushion correct technique or treat an injury?

No. Rowing cushions are seat and training accessories, not medical treatments or guaranteed solutions for pain, numbness, injury, or technique problems.

Sources: [Improve Rowing Machine Seat Fit and Support](/rowing-machine/how-to-reduce-rowing-machine-seat-discomfort)

## Policy Q&A

### What is Hornet's dragon boat paddle warranty?

Hornet provides a 3-year warranty on dragon boat paddles from the original purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not damage from use, impact, normal wear, misuse, handling, or transportation.

Sources: [Warranty and Shipping](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/warranty)

### What is Hornet's warranty on other products?

Hornet provides a 1-year warranty on products other than dragon boat paddles from the original purchase date.

Sources: [Warranty and Shipping](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/warranty)

### What is Hornet's return window?

Unused USA purchases made through Hornetwatersports.com may be returned or exchanged within 30 days of purchase with prior authorization. International purchases are not eligible for returns or exchanges.

Sources: [Returns and Exchanges](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/returns-exchanges)

### Are clearance items returnable?

No. Hornet clearance items are final sale and are not eligible for return, refund, or exchange.

Sources: [Returns and Exchanges](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/returns-exchanges)

### How quickly do in-stock Hornet orders ship?

Hornet states that in-stock orders ship by USPS or UPS within 1 to 2 business days after receipt, Monday through Friday.

Sources: [Warranty and Shipping](https://www.hornetwatersports.com/pages/warranty)
