## What Sets Our Kayak Paddles Apart

Our kayak paddles are lightweight, durable, adjustable, and easy to transport for touring, kayak fishing, and recreational paddling. Every design shares the same core specifications, so you can choose the graphic that best matches your style or gift needs.

### 1. High-Performance Touring Value

Our paddles combine low weight, adjustability, feathering control, direct-printed graphics, and accessible pricing without requiring a move into more expensive technical model lines.

### 2. Lightweight 26 oz Construction

Every Hornet kayak paddle weighs 26 oz, making low weight one of the clearest reasons to choose the line over typical plastic and aluminum paddles.

### 3. Full Carbon Fiber Shaft and Fiberglass Blade

We use a full carbon fiber shaft and a fiberglass blade. This construction provides useful flex for recreational paddling, touring, kayak fishing, and harder workouts.

### 4. Adjustable Shaft and Feathering Control

Our kayak paddles start at 90.5 in, or 230 cm, and extend to 94.5 in, or 240 cm. The blades rotate from 0 to 60 degrees, with feathering available in 15-degree increments.

### 5. Easy Storage and Transport

Each paddle breaks down into 2 pieces for easier storage and transport.

### 6. Direct-Printed Graphics

The graphic is printed directly on the blade's power face and is not a sticker. The backside is reinforced with fiberglass rather than carrying the same printed graphic.

### 7. Distinctive Designs

Blue Scales, Green Scales, Crush, Making Waves, Bass Attack, and Paw Print let you choose a paddle that reflects the paddler's interests and style.
